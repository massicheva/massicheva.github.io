Privacy policy

Playlist Generator is a personal tool run by its author on his own computer, for his own Google accounts only.

  • What it accesses: through the YouTube Data API, the playlists and liked videos of the author's own accounts, so it can read and update his playlists.
  • Where data goes: it stays on the author's computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties. The one exception is that artist and track names are sent to an AI model (via OpenRouter) to pick recommendations.
  • Other users: none. The tool does not collect data from anyone else.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
